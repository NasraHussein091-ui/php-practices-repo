# Assignment 1 - PHP

This folder contains my Assignment 1 PHP programs.

## Programs

### 1. commonfactore.php
Finds the HCF of two numbers.

### 2. Divisibility.php
Checks whether a number is divisible by 3, 5, both, or neither.

### 3. Divissible by 2 and 5.php
Prints numbers divisible by both 2 and 5.

### 4. greatest &smallest.php
Compares three numbers and finds the greatest and smallest number.

### 5. LCM.php
Finds the LCM of two numbers.

### 6. Multiplication Table.php
Creates a 12 × 12 multiplication table using loops.

### 7. odd and even.php
Prints odd and even numbers.

### 8. Prime numbers 10 to 50.php
Prints prime numbers from 10 to 50.

### 9. prime numbers and non brime.php
Checks whether a number is prime or not prime.

### 10. Reverse.php
Reverses a number.
